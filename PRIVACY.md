# Privacy Policy: Producer's Reference Grabber

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Producer's Reference Grabber ("the extension") lets you save timestamped notes about tracks you're listening to on YouTube and SoundCloud.

## What the extension stores
When you save a reference, the extension stores:
- the track or video title,
- the page link and the timestamp,
- the note and tags you type,
- the date and time you saved it.

All of this is kept **only on your own device** in Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`).

## What the extension does not do
- It does not send your saved references, notes or tags to the developer or to any server.
- It has no analytics, tracking, ads or accounts.
- It does not read your browsing history. It runs only on youtube.com and soundcloud.com pages, and only reads the current player time and track title when you choose to save.
- It does not sell or share any data with anyone.

## Third-party content
The extension's Library page can show YouTube thumbnails and embed YouTube or SoundCloud players so you can replay your saved moments. When these load, your browser connects directly to YouTube or SoundCloud, and their own privacy policies apply. The "Get audio" links open store search pages (Bandcamp, Beatport) in a new tab only when you click them.

## Exporting and deleting your data
You can export everything to a CSV file from the popup or Library page. You can delete any saved reference with its ✕ button. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data.
